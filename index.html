Our Story

On the rail since the 1950s

In a diner, "the rail" is where every order gets clipped for the kitchen. Ours has been running a long time.

1950s

Opens in Downtown Oviedo

The TownHouse opens its doors, serving down-home Southern cooking right in the heart of town.

For Decades

Becomes the Town's Table

Regulars fill the counter every morning. The TownHouse becomes a true Oviedo landmark — some say it practically was downtown Oviedo.

2015

Road Construction Forces a Move

A widening project at the heart of downtown displaces the original building. The TownHouse has to leave the spot it called home for decades.

2016

Reopens on Central Avenue

A new building goes up just a few hundred feet away — more seating, a patio on the Florida Trail, and the original sign built right into it.

Today

Same Place, Same Plates

Still family-run, still Southern cooking, still open breakfast through dinner, seven days a week.

What Hasn't Changed
